Subject: Organizational update

Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to operational excellence, it has been decided that we will be streamlining several functions over the coming quarter. This restructuring reflects the realities of a changing market and our need to remain efficient and competitive.

Approximately 140 roles will be impacted. Affected team members will be let go with a transition package, and their responsibilities will be realigned across existing teams to capture efficiencies. It is understood that this news is difficult.

These decisions were not taken lightly. Mistakes were made in how quickly we scaled, and the necessary corrections are now being implemented. We remain confident that this right-sizing positions us for sustainable, high-performing growth.

We need to stay focused and professional during this period. We ask that everyone keep it together, remain team players, and continue to deliver best-in-class results for our customers. Questions should be directed to your manager.

Thank you for your continued dedication.

Leadership
