Riverside Branch Library: Summer Hours Report

The Riverside Branch Library extended its weekday closing time from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. between June 3 and August 30. This report summarizes attendance during the extended hours and the cost of staffing them.

Attendance. Door counters recorded 4,212 visits between 6 p.m. and 8 p.m. across the 64 weekdays of the trial, an average of 66 visits per evening. Tuesday and Wednesday evenings were the busiest, averaging 81 visits each. Friday evenings averaged 39 visits.

Use. Staff logged 1,130 computer sessions and 2,480 checkouts during the extended hours. The homework help table, staffed by two volunteers, served 318 students.

Cost. The extra hours required 256 additional staff hours at a total cost of $9,472, paid from the branch's seasonal budget. No overtime was needed.

Feedback. Of 212 patrons who completed the comment card, 174 said they would use evening hours again, 22 said they would not, and 16 did not answer the question.

Options for the board. The board could keep the 8 p.m. closing time year-round, keep it only on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, or return to the 6 p.m. closing time. The branch manager will present cost estimates for each option at the September 12 meeting.
