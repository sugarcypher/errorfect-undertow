UNDERTOW

Hidden vector detector

The surface of a text says one thing; the current underneath moves you somewhere. Undertow finds the linguistic mechanisms that apply a hidden vector of belief (what you're moved to accept), action (what you're moved to do), or thought (how your thinking is shaped or shut down) — in a paragraph or across a whole body of work.

What Undertow does

Undertow reads a text, or a whole body of work, and marks the language that moves a reader toward a belief, an action, or a way of thinking. For each mechanism it shows the sentence it sits in, how it works, and a question you can put to it.

  • Scans of any length, run in your browser. Your text stays on your device.
  • Bodies of work: many documents at once, with the mechanisms they share, repeated phrasing and recurring targets.
  • Hidden layer: invisible characters, look-alike letters and embedded instructions to machines.
  • Deep reads with Claude: a written analysis of each hidden vector and what the claim rests on, up to 10 a day.
  • Reports in Markdown and JSON.

Undertow reports patterns in language, not intent. A flagged sentence is a place to look more closely, not a verdict on the writer.
